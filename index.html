Act 05 · The Lookbook

How it will look. How it will feel.

Working Title
"The Room"
Visual
Close on the details you've engineered: the book, the cups, the shredder, the cards. Wide enough to show a full room of men who chose to be there. Faces caught mid-conversation.
Pace
Unhurried through the talk, quicker through the moments. Built so the first two seconds work on a feed without a caption.
Sound
The ambience of the room carries it. Glasses, chairs, laughter, conversations overlapping, the shredder at work. Layered with care, so you hear the depth of everything going on as well as see it.
Structure
It opens on the pub before doors. The middle fills it. It closes on the membership cards and the invitation to the next room.
Voice
Your own voice carries the film. I'll pull you aside at two moments during the session, so the explanation is live, with the work happening behind you.
